October 20, 2024 · Avalon Hollywood

Fashion for Peace: Royal Majesty

Fashion for Peace brought the Royal Majesty showcase to Avalon Hollywood, featuring the Jacob Meir Collection and a historic collaboration with the African Textile Museum and founder-designer Queen Ahneva Ahneva.

David Mayorga produced Eyes of Frequency’s coverage and sent the team fully equipped with his production gear. That support made it possible to document the runway, red carpet, and guest experience while keeping the visual approach consistent across the evening.

The production captured a meeting point between textiles from five African countries, contemporary fashion, cultural history, and creative connection.

Fashion can carry history while creating a new shared moment.
